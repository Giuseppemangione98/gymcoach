Setblok Privacy

Support

Setblok is with you at the gym: follow your plan set by set, on iPhone and Apple Watch.

Got a problem or an idea? Use “Send feedback” in the app's Profile, or write to us with your phone model and iOS version.

Write to setblokapp@icloud.com

How do I create my plan?

When you first open the app, tap “Create your first plan”, add days and exercises, then set sets, reps and load.

Do I need Apple Watch or Health?

No. Apple Watch and Health access are optional. You can change permissions anytime in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Will I lose my data if I change iPhone?

Your data stays on your phone and doesn't pass through Setblok servers. To move it to a new iPhone, transfer content from your old phone, or use “Export backup” and “Restore backup” in the app's Settings.

How do I request a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Setblok replace a doctor or trainer?

No. Before starting a training program, or if you have doubts about your health, consult a doctor.
