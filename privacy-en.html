GymCoach Support

Privacy

Your data stays on your devices. GymCoach has no accounts, ads or analytics. The only thing that leaves the app is feedback you choose to send.

Last updated: October 31, 2026.

Data controller

Giuseppe Mangione. For any privacy request, write to gymcoachapp@icloud.com.

Data stored on your device

The app saves this information on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Name, photo and weight are optional.

  • first name, last name and profile photo
  • weight and progress photos
  • plans, workouts, loads and results

The developer cannot see this data. iPhone and Apple Watch exchange the information needed for your workout through Apple system services.

Apple Health data

GymCoach accesses Health only with your permission:

  • iPhone: reads your weight and saves workouts
  • Apple Watch: reads heart rate and active calories during a workout, and saves the workout and active calories

This data is used only to show your workout and progress. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app works even if you deny access.

Backups and Apple services

If iPhone backup is on, the system may include the app's data according to your Apple settings. Purchases, downloads and refunds are handled by Apple, which may give the developer aggregated statistics. Apple's privacy policy applies.

In-app feedback

If you send feedback from Profile, the app transmits to the controller's Google Forms form:

  • the type of report and the message you write
  • your email address, only if you choose to provide it
  • app version, iOS version, phone model and language

None of this is sent unless you use feedback. Google processes it on the controller's behalf, including outside the European Union, under its own privacy policy.

When you contact us

We use your feedback or email only to help you and improve the app. The legal basis is handling your request. We keep messages for as long as needed and delete them on request.

Deleting data and revoking permissions

  • Deleting GymCoach from iPhone and Apple Watch erases the data the app saved on those devices. Any backups remain until you delete them.
  • Workouts saved in Health are managed in the Health app.
  • To revoke access, go to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → GymCoach.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability of the personal data you sent us, and object to its processing. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of the page.
